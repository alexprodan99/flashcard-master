## 3. Architectural Health & Invariants Check

1. **Hexagonal Architecture (Ports & Adapters - AD-1, AD-2):**
   - Invariant strictly maintained: `packages/core` contains zero framework, DOM, or parser dependencies.
   - All external fetch, XML, Readability, and glob libraries are fully encapsulated inside `packages/adapters`.
2. **SQLite WAL Mode & Data Integrity (AD-3, NFR-201, NFR-302):**
   - SQLite client enforces `PRAGMA journal_mode = WAL` and `PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`.
   - All timestamp defaults standardized to ISO-8601 UTC strings.
3. **Fault Isolation (NFR-301):**
   - Network timeouts, HTTP 4xx/5xx, and malformed feed dates are caught at the strategy layer.
   - `IngestionService.syncAllActive()` continues processing sibling sources when an individual source encounters an error.
4. **Incremental Delta Tracking (FR-105, AD-8):**
   - Tested and verified: RSS GUID cursors and SHA-256 content hashes prevent duplicate document ingestion.

## 4. Key Learnings & Wins

- **Standardizing Date Utilities Early:** Real-world RSS feeds and web pages often contain malformed or out-of-range date strings. Creating a shared `safeIsoDate()` utility in `packages/adapters/src/utils/date.ts` prevented potential runtime `RangeError` exceptions during feed ingestion.
- **In-Memory Testing Speed:** Vitest tests against in-memory SQLite and mock fetch functions run in under 2 seconds across the entire repository.
- **Clean Story Scoping:** Decomposing ingestion into discrete strategy stories (1.3, 1.4, 1.5) before uniting them in 1.6 minimized cognitive overload and kept review loops fast.
