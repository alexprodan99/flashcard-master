## 1. Executive Summary

Epic 9 resolved the critical pedagogical trade-off between atomic micro-fact recall and deep architectural synthesis:
1. **Configurable Card Depth (`depthMode`):** Added 3 pedagogical modes (`atomic` rapid recall, `deep-dive` multi-concept mechanisms/tradeoffs, and `scenario` applied troubleshooting challenges).
2. **Dynamic Context Chunk Sizing (`chunkMultiplier`):** Enabled scaling text chunks from standard $1\times$ ($2\text{k}$ chars) up to $2\times$, $3\times$, $5\times$, and **Full Article Mode** ($0$ / unchunked single-pass) for macro-level concept synthesis across long documents.
3. **Dual Interface Calibration:** Added CLI flags (`--depth`, `--chunk-multiplier`) and reactive Svelte 5 visual controls (interactive radio cards and scope pills) in Generate Studio.
4. **Factual Grounding Invariant:** Guaranteed 100% verbatim quote grounding compliance across all depth modes.
