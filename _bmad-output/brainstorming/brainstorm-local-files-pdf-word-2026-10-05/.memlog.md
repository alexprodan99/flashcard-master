- (insight by user) Catastrophic failure: Severe lag, UI stutter, and recurring Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes completely degrading user experience during large document processing
- (idea by coach) Decouple upload from parsing: file upload writes to disk in milliseconds; background task worker parses pages incrementally with progress reporting and strict heap limits, preventing UI lag
- (idea by coach) Worst idea: Interleave two-column text and repeating page headers/footers. Flip: Use column-aware reading order extraction and regex/frequency deduplication to strip recurring headers, footers, and page numbers
